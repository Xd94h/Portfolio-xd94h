01Project Overview

Road Rush 3D is a lightweight, endless-runner style racing game rendered in 3D directly in the browser — no downloads, no plugins, just open the link and drive.

The player steers a car down an endless road while traffic, obstacles, and scenery scroll past, dodging cars and hazards to rack up score and survive as long as possible. Speed climbs the longer you last, raising the difficulty in real time.

A live HUD tracks level, score, lives, elapsed time, and current speed, and the game embeds a rating and review system so players can leave feedback directly on the page.
