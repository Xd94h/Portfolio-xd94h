01Project Overview

Passport Size TOOL solves a small but genuinely annoying problem — turning a single photo into a print-ready passport sheet without opening Photoshop. Most people either pay a studio for six duplicate prints or waste time manually aligning crops in an editor.

This tool cuts that down to three steps: upload a photo, crop it manually with full control (zoom, straighten, aspect ratio presets), then drop it into a PSD template where six boxes are auto-aligned with borders and padding calculated for you.

Everything runs entirely client-side — no backend, no server upload, no waiting. The output can be exported as PSD, PNG, JPG, PDF, or a full ZIP bundle, ready to send straight to print.

02How It Works
01

Upload the source photo

Pull an image straight from your device, paste one from clipboard (Ctrl+V), or import it via a direct link — whatever's fastest.

02

Crop it manually, precisely

A Photoshop-style crop editor with live preview: zoom, straighten by degree, drag-resize the crop box, and snap to presets — Passport 35×45mm, Square 1:1, Standard 3:4, or Landscape 4:3.

03

Drop into a PSD template

Upload a PSD with six passport boxes; placeholders are auto-detected from the layer structure and padding is auto-calculated — no manual alignment needed. Templates you upload are saved permanently in-browser for reuse.

04

Generate the final sheet

One click produces the finished six-up sheet, ready to download as PSD, PNG, JPG, PDF, or a full ZIP — with a fullscreen preview before you commit.

03Key Features
Zx

Manual Crop Editor

Zoom, straighten, drag-resize, and rotate in 90° steps with keyboard shortcuts for speed.

Ar

Aspect Ratio Presets

Passport 35×45mm, Square, Standard 3:4, and Landscape 4:3 built in, plus a free-form mode.

6x

Six-Up Auto Layout

Each cropped photo fills a full row of six boxes in order — extra rows hide their borders automatically.

Ps

PSD-Native Templates

Reads real PSD layer structure to auto-detect placeholders and calculate padding.

Lc

Local, Persistent Storage

Saved PSDs and preferences live in your browser permanently — no account, no re-uploading.

Ex

Multi-Format Export

Download the finished sheet as PSD, PNG, JPG, PDF, or a bundled ZIP of everything at once.

04Built Client-Side

No backend server — the entire crop-to-export pipeline, including PSD parsing and rendering, runs in the browser. Admin panels and template libraries were deliberately left out of this client-only build.
